About Anathema Translator

Anathema Translator is a tool that transforms normal text into glitchy, corrupted text that appears to be breaking down or being translated through some forbidden means.

It uses various techniques to create glitchy text effects:

  • Character substitution with similar-looking glyphs
  • Special symbols and mathematical notations
  • Text reversal and inversion
  • Diacritical marks and combining characters
  • Vertical text arrangements

Adjust the settings to control how much and what type of corruption is applied to your text. Save your favorite outputs to reference later.

Perfect for creating eerie text effects for stories, games, or just to add a touch of digital corruption to your messages.
